## Cross-cutting acceptance checks

- English and Arabic text is present and consistent for all bilingual requirements.
- API authorization is tested independently of portal/mobile navigation.
- The communications navigation mismatch is resolved rather than relied upon as an accidental guard.
- The I4 audit records both owner-only exceptions separately: ownership-change initiation and T10 tenancy approval.
- B5 is not closed solely by D1’s prior automated acceptance.
- B4 is referenced as Stage 2b work and is not rebuilt in Stage 4.
- Stage 4b document-library features remain out of scope.
- The final delivery report will list test-count deltas, deviations, acceptance criteria, and evidence boundaries.

## Evidence package planned after implementation

The completed stage will publish revisioned individual files under `evidence/stage-4/`, including the delivery report, test/UAT records, any required companion evidence, and a manifest containing SHA-256 values computed from the exact pushed GitHub blobs and the evidence-content commit ID. Earlier revisions will remain untouched.
