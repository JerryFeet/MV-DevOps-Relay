## H2 precondition — explicit decision (b)

The approved H2 rule is deliberately **(b)**:

> The unit must hold at least one active Waha Pass credential. Any resident of
> that unit who has portal access and a completed resident record linked to the
> unit may request a Guest Day Pass. The requester does **not** need to
> personally hold either of the unit’s two credentials.

This is a unit-level Waha requirement, not an individual-credential
requirement. It is intentionally different from F7 facility booking, where the
individual credential check remains applicable.

All other terms in the r1 Phase 0 contract remain unchanged, including H1’s
removal of Waha and four-guest quota checks from ordinary guest registration,
the `pending_payment` → `paid` → `issued` lifecycle, and issuance only from a
verified callback purpose handler.
